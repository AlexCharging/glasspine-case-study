Case study · GROW · Submission note

Increasing new resident activation with Glasspine Guide

Objective

Increase the proportion of new residents who become active users of Glasspine Resident within their first 14 days.

Problem & Hypothesis

New residents struggle at decision moments: interpreting charges, identifying emergency maintenance and understanding lease options. I hypothesise that introducing Glasspine Guide through a post-onboarding checklist with three contextual questions will increase meaningful resident activation within 14 days.

Proposed Solution

The prototype compares an assumed standard dashboard against a Guide-first checklist. The three prompts map directly to the case study: explain an itemised bill, triage a maintenance issue safely, and retrieve relevant lease terms for a life change.

Each scripted answer shows a property-specific source and an appropriate next step. The maintenance journey continues into a simulated request form and confirmation; billing and lease examples focus on transparent explanations and informed action.

Measurement

I would A/B test the checklist against the standard dashboard. The primary metric is 14-day meaningful activation, defined as completing at least one qualifying resident task.

Supporting metrics include Guide engagement, completion rate, number of logins, time to first meaningful action and subsequent platform engagement.

Decisions & Trade-offs

I prioritised clearer discovery of existing Guide and resident workflows over new features. I chose one fully connected maintenance journey and two shorter contextual examples to keep the prototype focused.

All data, clauses, responses and interactions are fictional. Real AI, integrations, legal verification and emergency contact configuration are outside prototype scope.

The prototype uses simulated interactions and data; production integration, personalisation and broader workflows are deliberately outside scope.
