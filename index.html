Case study · GROW · Submission note

Increasing new resident activation with Glasspine Guide

Objective

Increase the proportion of new residents who become active users of Glasspine Resident within their first 14 days.

Problem & Hypothesis

New residents may struggle to discover relevant functionality after onboarding, limiting their understanding of the platform's value. I hypothesise that introducing Glasspine Guide through a post-onboarding checklist, with three suggested questions, will encourage residents to engage and complete their first meaningful task.

Proposed Solution

The prototype compares an assumed existing dashboard with a Guide-first experience. Residents are prompted to explore three common questions, with Guide providing contextual answers and directing them towards relevant functionality.

The prototype demonstrates one complete journey: a resident asks about maintenance, receives guidance, submits a repair request and sees confirmation. This illustrates how Guide can move beyond answering questions to helping residents complete valuable actions.

Measurement

I would run an A/B test comparing the existing experience against the Guide-first checklist.

The primary metric is 14-day meaningful activation, defined as completing at least one qualifying resident task. Supporting metrics include Guide engagement, task completion, time to first meaningful action and subsequent platform engagement. Guardrails include answer accuracy, incorrect submissions and support contacts.

Decisions & Trade-offs

I prioritised improving discoverability of an existing capability over building new functionality. I also chose one complete journey rather than multiple incomplete workflows.

The three suggested questions are illustrative and would require validation using resident behaviour and support data. AI responses must be grounded in property-specific information, with escalation available when guidance is uncertain.

The prototype uses simulated interactions and data; production integration, personalisation and broader workflows are deliberately outside scope.
